An immersive-vr session feeds the engine your head pose every frame, so a source stays where it is in the room while you turn. That is the one thing the flat playground cannot show you.

A browser resumes an AudioContext only from a user gesture, so nothing can make a sound until you press a button. Put headphones on first: every scene is rendered for two ears.
